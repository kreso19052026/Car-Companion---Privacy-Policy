Privacy Policy

App: Car Companion (com.kozotechnology.carcompanion)
Effective date : September 8, 2026

Car Companion ("the App", "we", "us") is developed by Kozo Technology. It is an automatic drive recorder: it detects your car over Bluetooth and logs each drive from your phone's own GPS and motion sensors. It also has a full-screen "Live View" that displays real-time driving figures while your phone is mounted. There is no account and no sign-in.

What stays on your device

  • Your trips — routes, distances, times, speeds, driving-event data and scores are stored only on your phone and are deleted when you uninstall the App. They are never uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • Bluetooth device selection — the name and address of the device you pick as your car, used only to know when a drive starts and ends.
  • Settings and statistics — kept locally for the Insights screen.
  • Live View — the real-time position, speed and motion shown on the Live View screen are displayed only. They are not written to your trip history and are not sent anywhere.
  • Exports — when you choose to export a trip (PDF, CSV, GPX and other formats), the file is created on demand and handed to the Android share sheet. You choose where it goes; the App does not upload it.

What is sent, and only if you opt in

If you leave "Fetch weather & place names" on (Settings), then for a finished trip the App sends approximate coordinates rounded to about 100 m and a timestamp to:

  • Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com) — to get the weather at the trip.
  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim (openstreetmap.org) — to turn the start/end points into place names, as a fallback to your phone's built-in geocoder.

If you turn on "Road speed-limit lookup" (off by default), the App sends the bounding box of a finished route to the OpenStreetMap Overpass API to fetch posted speed limits.

When you open a route map, the OpenStreetMap tile server receives the map-tile coordinates needed to draw it.

These requests carry no identifiers and no advertising ID. The services do not retain the data. Turn the enrichment setting off and the App makes no network requests except ad requests.

Advertising

The App uses Google AdMob to show a native ad in the Insights screen and an optional rewarded video ad (watch one to extend the free period). AdMob may collect an advertising identifier and related data (device information, coarse location derived from IP, ad interactions) as described in Google's Privacy Policy. On first launch the App shows Google's consent form where required. We do not sell or share any other data, and we receive no personal data from AdMob.

Permissions

  • Bluetooth — detect your car connecting and disconnecting.
  • Location (foreground only) — record the drive's GPS route and power the Live View while its screen is open. The App never requests "Allow all the time".
  • Physical activity (optional) — skip trips where you were a passenger.
  • Notifications — show the recording status and optional reminders.
  • Ignore battery optimizations (optional) — keep the recorder alive for the whole drive.
  • On Android 12+ you may link your car as a companion device so Android can wake the App reliably when the car connects.

Data retention and deletion

All trip data stays on the device until you delete it. Delete an individual trip from its detail screen; permanently remove deleted trips and raw motion logs from Settings → Advanced capture → "Storage used by trips". Uninstalling the App, or clearing its storage in Android Settings, removes everything. Because there is no server, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children.
